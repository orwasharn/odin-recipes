Fish-stew

Description

A delicious Kenyan-style fish stew made with fresh fish, juicy tomatoes, onions, and aromatic spices.

Cooked in a rich, flavorful gravy and garnished with fresh dhania, it's perfect served with ugali, rice, or chapati.

ingredients

  • 1 medium fried fish (tilapia)
  • 2 medium tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • tablespoon cooking oil
  • small green pepper (optional)
  • 1 clove garlic, crushed (optional)
  • ½ teaspoon curry powder (optional)
  • Salt to taste
  • ½ cup water
  • A little coriander (dhania), chopped
  • Lemon juice (optional)

Steps

  1. Heat oil in a sufuria.
  2. Fry onions until soft.
  3. Add tomatoes and cook until soft.
  4. Add water, salt, and spices.
  5. Add the fried fish and simmer for 10-15 minutes.
  6. Garnish with dhania and serve hot.
