chicken-curry

Description

Chicken curry is a flavorful dish prepared with chicken, tomatoes, onions and spices. It can be served with rice, chapati or other accompaniments.

This simple recipe makes a warm and satisfying meal using ingredients that are easy to find.

ingredients

  • 500 g chicken, cut into pieces
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 2 cloves of garlic, minced
  • 1 teaspoon curry powder
  • 2 tablespoons cooking oil
  • Salt to taste
  • Water as needed

Steps

  1. Heat cooking oil in a pot and fry the onion until soft.
  2. Add garlic and curry powder, stirring briefly.
  3. Add the chicken pieces and cook until lightly browned.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, salt and a little water.
  5. Cover and simmer until the chicken is thoroughly cooked and tender.
  6. Serve hot with rice or chapati.
