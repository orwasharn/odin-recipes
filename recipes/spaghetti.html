Spaghetti

Spaghetti is a delicious Italian-inspired meal made with pasta and a rich tomato and minced meat sauce. It is a satisfying dish suitable for lunch or dinner.

This recipe combines simple ingredients to create a flavorful meal that can be enjoyed with family and friends.

ingredients

  • 200g spaghetti
  • 200g mincedbeef
  • 2 chopped tomatoes
  • 1 chopped bulb onion
  • 2 minced garlic
  • 2 tablespoon of cooking oil
  • salt and pepper to taste
  • water for boiling the pasta

Steps

  1. Boil water in a pot and cook the spaghetti according to the package instructions.
  2. Heat cooking oil in a pan and fry the chopped onion until soft.
  3. Add the garlic and minced beef, then cook until the meat is browned.
  4. Add the chopped tomatoes, salt and pepper. Simmer until the sauce thickens and the meat is thoroughly cooked.
  5. Drain the spaghetti and serve it with the prepared sauce.
